      Supporting processes

      The fill itself takes seconds per vial. Most of the sterility assurance comes from the systems around it: cleaning, sterilising, decontaminating, the water and steam they use, and the air and monitoring that prove the critical zone stayed clean.

      Where the efficiency comes from

      Illustrative simulation. Line rate, counts, weights and particle readings are generated, and changeover phases run in time-lapse with typical real durations shown beside them. Parameters follow common practice and EU GMP Annex 1 (2022); a real line runs to its own validated values.
