      Regulations and what they require

      A sterile injectable sold in Europe and the United States has to satisfy two rule books that agree on intent but differ in wording and in a few numbers. EU GMP Annex 1, revised in 2022, is shared word for word with PIC/S and closely mirrored by WHO, so it reaches well beyond Europe. FDA works from 21 CFR Part 211, its 2004 aseptic processing guidance and the USP chapters.

      Clean-room limits side by side

      EU figures: Annex 1 (2022) Table 1 (classification), Table 5 (total particle monitoring) and Table 6 (viable action limits). The ≥ 5.0 µm limit of 29/m³ in Grade A is a monitoring limit for trending, not a classification limit. FDA figures are the in-operation action levels in Table 1 of the 2004 aseptic processing guidance. Viable limits are CFU per m³ of air, per 90 mm settle plate over 4 h, and per 55 mm contact plate or glove print.

      Supporting processes

      The fill itself takes seconds per vial. Most of the sterility assurance comes from the systems around it: cleaning, sterilising, decontaminating, the water and steam they use, and the air and monitoring that prove the critical zone stayed clean.
