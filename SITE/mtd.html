Am I in Making Tax Digital, and when?

For UK sole traders and landlords. Enter your gross income and find out when MTD for Income Tax starts for you and when your first quarterly update is due.

How it works

Who's in

Sole traders and landlords whose self-employment and property income together is more than £50,000 from April 2026, £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. Gross income counts, not profit.

Which year counts

HMRC looks at your tax return for the year two years earlier: 2024-25 income decides April 2026, 2025-26 decides April 2027.

What you then do

Keep digital records and send a quarterly update by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May, then a final declaration by 31 January. Spreadsheets are allowed with bridging software.

A guide, not tax advice. Some people are exempt or can apply to be, and a share of jointly owned property counts only as your share. Check your own position on gov.uk.
