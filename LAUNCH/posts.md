**Title:** Launching a $19 spreadsheet kit with a free browser tool as the funnel. No server, $0
running costs

**Body:**

The plan was a product I could put out without paying for anything every month:

- **The product:** four spreadsheet templates for freelancers (invoice, expenses, tax set-aside,
  client/project tracker) plus a PDF guide, sold as a download for $19.
- **The funnel:** a free invoice generator on GitHub Pages. Static HTML/JS, works offline, stores
  nothing on a server. It's useful on its own and links to the kit.
- **The cost:** $0 a month. Hosting is free, the store takes its cut per sale.

Some things I learned:

- Making spreadsheets work in Excel, Google Sheets *and* LibreOffice rules out a lot: no XLOOKUP,
  FILTER or dynamic arrays. SUMIFS and INDEX/MATCH everywhere.
- The templates are generated by code, not hand-made, so every formula is tested by recalculating
  the files in LibreOffice.
- Most of it was built by AI coding agents working in parallel from a plan, with me making the
  calls.

Revenue so far: $0, this is launch day. I'll post numbers once there are some.
