# Market brief: Freelancer Finance Kit

October 2026. Store pages (Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip), gov.uk and Reddit were blocked by the proxy, so
every figure below comes from search-engine summaries of those pages, not from reading them.
Treat prices and counts as spot checks, not a survey. **[impression]** marks judgement that is
not backed by a source.

## Verdict

A generic "freelancer finance kit" can't compete. The category is crowded, cheap, and buyers search
by country and tax form, so a kit with no country loses on every search a buyer actually types. The
free invoice generator won't pull traffic either: it goes up against established free tools.

**Recommended positioning: the UK sole trader kit for Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax.**
It would sell as spreadsheets plus bridging software, invoicing included, with no subscription. The
target buyers are the £30k–£50k cohort, who must keep digital records and file quarterly from April
2027.

## Where it sells and what it costs

- **Etsy** is where buyers search for these templates. Its fees are about 6.5% plus a listing fee
  [5]. Etsy guides call budget and finance templates high-competition, and say niche templates
  (by trade or country) face less competition and sell at higher prices [3].
- **Gumroad** charges 10% + $0.50 on direct sales and 30% on sales from its Discover marketplace
  [5]. Its sellers in this space are single-purpose US tax trackers [2]. **[impression]** Gumroad
  sells to traffic you bring yourself. It isn't a place buyers browse.
- **Prices:**
  - Freelance and small-business finance bundles on Etsy run from $0.99 to about $16. Most show
    a permanent "sale" against an inflated original price [1].
  - US Schedule C / 1099 trackers cost $4.99–$19, and the better ones say they were built by tax
    professionals [4].
  - UK self-assessment and MTD trackers cost about £4–£21. One MTD listing sells at £9.59 [7][8].
  - Free UK sole-trader and MTD spreadsheets also exist [8][9].
- **Search vs supply:** "freelancer template" gets about 320 searches a month on Etsy, against
  14,745 competing listings [6].

## What the best sellers offer

- Country-specific tax maths: US SE tax with the current year's IRS rates; UK income tax, Class 4
  NI and payments on account. Categories map to the tax form (Schedule C, or SA103 boxes)
  [2][4][7].
- The tax year in the title ("2026-27", "2026"), and both Excel and Google Sheets in one listing
  [2][3][7].
- Niching by trade, down to single professions such as a "Private Tutor UK Tax Spreadsheet" [7].

## What reviews say

Reviews couldn't be read directly. The search snippets that were visible praise ease of use, looks,
included instructions and a seller who answers fast. The one complaint seen was a broken date
formula, which the seller fixed [10]. Sellers' FAQs single out formulas that break between Google
Sheets and Excel as the common failure [10]. **[impression]** Cross-app reliability, which the kit
designs for, is a real selling point, but only once it's verified in Excel and Sheets.

## Where the kit stands

- Strengths:
  - Four connected files. Competitors in the tax niches mostly sell single trackers
    **[impression, from listing titles]**.
  - No macros, and designed for all three apps.
  - The free generator.
- Weaknesses against the market:
  - The tax set-aside uses rates the buyer chooses. Every competitor that sells on tax computes
    the real liability. The kit's weakest component sits in the niche's most important slot.
  - "Any currency", with no tax year and no tax form, matches no buyer search.
  - At $19 it is above the median of the range, with no reviews.
  - The invoice generator is up against invoice-generator.com (about 479K in search traffic) and
    Zoho Invoice, which is now free for up to 500 invoices a year [11]. **[impression]** It will
    not be found by search on its own.

## Why UK MTD rather than US 1099

- **A forcing event with dates.** MTD for Income Tax started on 6 April 2026 for gross income above
  £50k. It reaches £30k from April 2027 and £20k from April 2028: nearly three million people by
  2028 [12]. They must keep digital records and send quarterly updates through recognised
  software. The update deadlines are 7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb and 7 May [12].
- **Spreadsheets are explicitly allowed** when paired with bridging software [13]. Several bridging
  tools read Excel or Google Sheets: MTD Companion (free to view, paid to submit), 100PcMTD (free
  for 6 months, then £30 a year), AbraTax and Abridge [14]. A buyer who refuses a subscription
  bookkeeping app needs a spreadsheet laid out correctly. That is this kit's "no subscription"
  pitch, now with a deadline behind it.
- **The US is bigger but has no deadline.** Its 1099 niche is already dense with sellers who claim
  tax-professional authorship [4]. **[impression]** A new shop has nothing to stand on there.
- The UK niche is not empty: there are MTD shops, £9.59 listings and free templates [7][8][9]. The
  case for the kit is the bundle (invoice, clients and MTD records in one) and the timing (ready
  before the 2027 cohort starts looking, roughly January to July 2027 **[impression]**). It is not
  that the niche lacks competitors.

## Changes this implies

**Kit (TOOLKIT/)**

- **Expenses:**
  - Default categories become the SA103 expense categories, still editable.
  - The tax year starts on 6 April, with MTD quarters.
  - Add mileage at HMRC's simplified rate (55p per mile for 2026-27 per [8]; verify on gov.uk) and
    the use-of-home flat rate.
- **Add an MTD quarterly sheet:** cumulative income and expense totals per SA103 category for each
  update period, laid out as one fixed range that bridging software can map. Test it against at
  least one bridging tool (100PcMTD's free trial or MTD Companion) before claiming "MTD-ready".
- **Tax:** replace the user-chosen rates with a real UK estimate: income tax bands, personal
  allowance taper, Class 4 NI and payments on account (31 Jan and 31 Jul). Keep the rates in a
  dated rates table that is updated every April. The "not tax advice" line stays.
- **One income record:** paid invoices in the client tracker should feed the MTD income figures, so
  the buyer doesn't enter income twice.
- **Invoice:** £ by default, UK date format, an optional VAT number line.
- **Guide:** add "Am I in MTD?" (the gross-income test), how to connect a bridging tool, and the
  quarterly calendar.
- **Before launch:** check every file in Excel and Google Sheets. That check is already a KIT.1
  condition, and it now carries the product claim.

**Site (SITE/)**

- Lead the landing page with MTD for UK sole traders, not "freelancers".
- The generator defaults to £ and UK invoice fields.
- Add a free "Does MTD apply to me, and when?" checker. It asks for gross income and gives the
  start date and first deadline. **[impression]** It is a better traffic magnet in this niche than
  one more invoice generator.

**Listing (LAUNCH/)**

- Make Etsy the primary store and Gumroad optional.
- New title: "UK Sole Trader MTD Spreadsheet Kit 2026-27: Bookkeeping, Invoice, Tax & NI
  Estimator, Excel & Google Sheets".
- Price: about £14 flat. No fake "60% off" sale. The launch code stays.
- Tags: MTD, making tax digital, sole trader, self assessment, HMRC, SA103, UK tax spreadsheet,
  bridging software, self employed UK.
- Posts: drop r/SideProject-style framing and target UK self-employed communities, where their
  rules allow it.

**Risks**

- Tax maths for one country must be correct and updated every April. That is a recurring cost the
  generic kit didn't have.
- A wrong "MTD-ready" claim is worse than none. The claim needs a real bridging-tool test and a
  reading of HMRC's own guidance, which this brief couldn't fetch.
- If the User wants to stay country-neutral, the honest fallback is to sell the kit cheaply as a
  generic bundle (around $9) and expect little: there is no niche and no search demand to rank for.
