# Selling Loopstone

## Why this game

- **Surakarta** is a traditional Javanese game: public domain, no licence or royalty. "Loopstone",
  the art, music, code and characters are original. The setting is a Maya-inspired temple world
  (invented characters, no real deities or copied glyphs), which gives it a strong look for store
  pages without tying it to any licensed property. Do a trademark search on "Loopstone" in your markets
  before paying for store listings.
- Almost no polished digital version exists on Steam, the App Store or Google Play, so the
  search term "Surakarta" and the "abstract strategy" niche are wide open.
- The loop-capture is unique and visual. A stone sweeping through four loops to take a piece is the
  kind of moment that sells in a 15-second clip.
- Buyers of this kind of game (Hive, Onitama and Santorini digital editions, chess variants) pay
  for a one-time premium game with no ads. Those games sell at $4.99 to $9.99.

## Business model: premium with a free demo

| Build | What's in it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Demo (`dist/web`) | Tutorial, Beginner's Path, coach, Temple Ascent steps 1–3, Quick Play Beginner–Medium, Pass & Play, 2 boards | Your website, itch.io free page, Steam demo |
| Full (`dist/full`) | All 8 pyramid steps, Hard and Master AI, all 4 boards | Paid on each store |

Suggested prices: **$4.99 on Steam, $5–7 on itch.io (pay-what-you-want minimum), $2.99 on mobile**.
No ads and no in-app purchases in v1 keeps reviews friendly and store review simple. A later
option is to make the mobile app free with one $2.99 "Unlock full game" in-app purchase. The
`FULL_VERSION` switch is where that check would go.

## Launch order (cheapest first)

1. **itch.io** (free to list, sells HTML5 games directly). Run `node build.mjs`, zip the
   *contents* of `dist/full`, upload as a paid HTML/downloadable game. Upload `dist/web` as a
   separate free "Loopstone Demo" page that links to the paid one. Put that page's URL in
   `node build.mjs <url>` so the demo's buy buttons point at it.
2. **Your own site**: the demo is deployed to GitHub Pages at `/game/` (see
   `.github/workflows/pages.yml`).
3. **Google Play** ($25 one-time). Wrap `dist/full` with Capacitor
   (`npm i @capacitor/core @capacitor/cli @capacitor/android`, `npx cap init`, set `webDir` to
   `dist/full`, `npx cap add android`, build an AAB in Android Studio). Alternatively publish the
   PWA as a Trusted Web Activity with Bubblewrap.
4. **Apple App Store** ($99/year, needs a Mac). Same Capacitor project, `npx cap add ios`.
5. **Steam** ($100 per app, recouped after $1,000 in sales). Wrap `dist/full` with Electron or
   Tauri. A Steam page should go up 2–3 months before release to collect wishlists. Ship the demo
   for Steam Next Fest.

## Store listing copy

**Short:** Jade against obsidian on a temple stone. Every capture rides the loops. Easy to learn, hard to master.

**Long:**
Slide, loop, strike. Loopstone brings a centuries-old strategy game to an ancient temple in the
jungle, where jade and obsidian stones meet on a carved limestone board.

Moving is simple: one step in any direction. Capturing is where it gets wild. Your stone races
along the inlaid lines and swings around the great corner loops to strike an enemy stone from
across the board. Every line is a threat, and every loop is a chance.

- **Temple Ascent:** climb the pyramid past 8 rivals, from the Novice Scribe to the Serpent King, each with their own portrait and voice
- **Quick Play** against 5 AI levels, from Beginner to a Master that plans many moves ahead
- **Pass & Play** with a friend on one device
- **Interactive tutorial**: learn in two minutes
- **Beginner's Path**: 7 strategy tips, each with a puzzle, to go from knowing the rules to winning games
- **Coach** that comments on your games: free captures, stones in danger, when to play safe
- Original flute, slit-drum and rattle soundtrack that never repeats
- Capture-path previews and threat warnings, so beginners learn fast
- 4 unlockable boards: Limestone, Jungle, Night of Venus, Cinnabar
- No ads, no timers, no internet needed

**Tags:** board game, strategy, abstract, ancient, maya, puzzle, chess-like, traditional, relaxing, singleplayer, local multiplayer

**Screenshots:** `store/`. Record a 15–30 s trailer of `04-lesson` → the four-loop capture in
`05-loop-ride` → a Temple Ascent victory, with the soundtrack.

## Content ideas for v1.1 (reasons to come back, review hooks)

- Daily puzzle: "capture in 2" positions. These can be generated by searching self-play games
  with `ai.js`.
- Online play (needs a server, so a running cost; only once sales justify it).
- Achievements (Steam/Play Games) for long loop captures, flawless wins and the like.
