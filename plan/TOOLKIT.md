# The paid kit

The product itself: spreadsheet templates that work in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice, plus a
short quick-start guide, zipped as the file the buyer downloads.

- 1 Spreadsheet templates
  - Goal:   Four polished .xlsx templates with working formulas and sample data: Invoice, Expense
            tracker (categories, monthly totals), Tax set-aside calculator (income in, % to put
            aside, quarterly view), Client & project tracker (rates, hours, unpaid balances).
            Generated by a script so they can be rebuilt.
  - Status: not started
- 2 Quick-start guide and bundle
  - Goal:       A short PDF guide for buyers and a build step producing `dist/freelancer-finance-kit.zip`.
  - Status:     not started
  - Depends on: 1
